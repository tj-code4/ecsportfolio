My Sport

  • Lacrosse is the sport I love because you need speed, skill, and teamwork and it always keeps me motivated. My coaches help me improve and help me understand the game on a deeper level, and my teammates make every practice and game so much more fun. I enjoy the challenge of learning new skills, communicating on the field, and giving everything I have for the team. The energy of the game, the support, and the feeling of growing as a player are all reasons why lacrosse means so much to me.

Tom Schreiber

  • Tom Schreiber is one of the best lacrosse players today, he is smart and skilled on the field. He plays midfield and is known for making hard plays. What I really like about him is how calm and confident he stays, even in tough moments, he makes good decisions which is something I want to get better at. He plays on the Archers which is a PLL team found in Utah.
