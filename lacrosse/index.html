My Sport

  • Lacrosse is the sport I love because you need speed, skill, and teamwork and it always keeps me motivated. My coaches help me improve and help me understand the game on a deeper level, and my teammates make every practice and game so much more fun. I enjoy the challenge of learning new skills, communicating on the field, and giving everything I have for the team. The energy of the game, the support, and the feeling of growing as a player are all reasons why lacrosse means so much to me.

Ever since then, I played football and a lacrosse and enjoyed both. In middle school I joined my first club lacrosse team, 801 Tribe improved my skills and we have grown so much as a team, I still play with them today. Once I reached highschool, the game grew on me and it became the only sport I played. I hope to continue playing lacrosse in college.

The Game Of Lacrosse

  • Lacrosse is played with two teams trying to score by getting a small rubber ball into the other team’s goal using a stick with a net on the end. The game moves fast, with players running up and down the field, passing, catching, and dodging defenders to create scoring chances. Each team has attackers, midfielders, defenders, and a goalie, and everyone has a specific job, whether it’s trying to score, protect the goal, or help with both.
